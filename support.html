Frequently Asked Questions

Farrow

Q: How do my kids join our family?

On your phone, open the Family tab and tap Invite next to your kid's name (or Invite Code at the top). On their device, open Farrow, sign in, choose Join a Family, enter the code and pick their name. Kids don't need their own device — you can run everything from your phone.

Q: My child is under 13 and can't use Sign in with Apple.

Apple blocks Sign in with Apple on child Apple IDs. Use email or Continue without Apple ID instead, then join with the invite code.

Q: How do I add a second parent?

Family tab → Invite a Parent shows a separate parent code. Kids never see it, so a kid can't join as a parent.

Q: Does Farrow hold or move real money?

No. Farrow tracks play-money balances for your family. It never connects to a bank or card.

Q: Someone made a mistake — how do I undo it?

Swipe left on the line in a kid's history and tap Delete. Deleting reverses its effect on the balance — including sending money back between siblings or cancelling a loan that hasn't been paid on yet. To let a kid undo their own mistakes, turn on Let this kid delete their own entries in Edit.

Q: How do I delete my family's data?

Account → Delete Account. If you're the last parent, the whole family is deleted, including every kid, history line and photo. To remove one kid, open their page → Edit → Remove Kid. See the Farrow Privacy Policy for details.

PropaneScout

Q: How are propane prices determined?

Prices are reported by users in the community. We do not set or guarantee any prices shown in the app.

Q: Why don't I see any locations near me?

PropaneScout relies on community-reported data. If your area doesn't have reports yet, you can be the first to contribute!

Alarmony

Q: Why isn't my alarm playing music?

Make sure you have an active Apple Music subscription and that the app has permission to access Apple Music in Settings > Alarmony.

Custom Interval Workout Timer

Q: Does the timer work on Apple Watch?

Yes! The Apple Watch companion app mirrors your timer and provides haptic alerts on your wrist.

General

Q: How do I restore my purchases?

Go to the app's settings and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account used for the original purchase.

Q: How do I delete my data?

For most of our apps, data is stored only on your device, and uninstalling the app removes it. Apps with accounts (like Farrow) have a Delete Account option inside the app — see that app's section above.
