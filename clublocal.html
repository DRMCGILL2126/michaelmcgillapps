Townimo

A marketplace for local subscription clubs — coffee sip clubs, car washes, studios, and more. Businesses create plans; members discover nearby and redeem with a pass.

← Michael McGill Apps

For customers

Browse subscriptions near you, subscribe in seconds, and keep every membership pass in one wallet. Order or claim what’s covered when you want to pick up.

For businesses

Create any plan you want, show a counter QR for signup, and get paid monthly through Stripe Connect. Townimo takes a small percent of subscription revenue.
