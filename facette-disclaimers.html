How Facette Works — and Its Limits

Facette uses your iPhone's built-in TrueDepth camera — the same camera used for Face ID — together with Apple's ARKit face-tracking software. This technology produces an approximate digital model (a "mesh") of your face. It was designed for consumer features like unlocking your phone and animating characters, not for precise measurement or any medical purpose.

Because of how this technology works, Facette's renderings and numbers are estimates, not exact measurements. They can be affected — sometimes significantly — by lighting, distance, camera angle, movement, facial expression, makeup, hair, skin condition, software updates, and the limitations of the device itself. Different scans of the very same face can produce different results. Facette may contain bugs or errors and may produce inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading output. We do not warrant that any result is accurate.
