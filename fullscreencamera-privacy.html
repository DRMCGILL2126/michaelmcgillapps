Full Screen Camera Pro — Privacy Policy

Effective: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Full Screen Camera Pro ("we", "us", "the app") handles information. The short version: the app has no servers, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your photos and videos go straight to your own photo library and are never sent to us.

Information We Collect

None. We do not collect, receive, or store your photos, videos, location, name, email address, contacts, device identifiers, usage analytics, or crash reports. The app contains no advertising or analytics software.

Camera and Microphone

The app uses your camera to show the viewfinder and take photos and videos, and your microphone to record sound with videos and Live Photos. Camera and microphone data is processed on your device only.

Photo Library

The app asks for permission only to add photos and videos to your library. It cannot see, read, or change anything already in your library.

So you can quickly review your shots, the app keeps temporary copies of photos and videos taken during the current session in its private storage on your device. These copies are deleted automatically the next time the app starts.

Location (Optional)

"Save Location" is off by default. If you turn it on, the app asks for location permission while in use and adds the location to the photos and videos you take, the same way the built-in Camera app does. The location is stored only in your photos' metadata in your own library; it is never sent to us. Keep in mind that when you share a photo, its location may be shared with it. You can remove it using the Options button in the iOS share sheet, or turn off Save Location in the app's Settings at any time.

Lock Screen Capture

When you open the camera from the Lock Screen, Camera Control, or the Action Button while your iPhone is locked, iOS doesn't allow the app to write to your photo library. Photos and videos you take are kept in a private folder managed by iOS until you next unlock and open the app, which then moves them into your library.

Settings

Your camera preferences (such as default mode, flash, and aspect ratio) are stored on your device only and are removed if you delete the app.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are purchased and managed through your Apple Account. Apple processes all payments; we never receive your payment information. The app checks your subscription status with Apple's StoreKit on your device. You can manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions.

Contacting Us

If you email us (for example, using Send Feedback in the app), we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. The feedback email is pre-filled with the app version and iOS version to help us answer. We use this only to reply to you.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties. There are no advertisers, data brokers, or third-party SDKs in the app.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, the new version will appear at this address with a new effective date.
