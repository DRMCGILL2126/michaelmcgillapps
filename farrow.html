Farrow
The Family Piggy Bank · iPhone & Apple Watch

Teach kids real money habits — one allowance at a time.

Farrow turns your family's piggy bank into an app. Track every kid's balance, pay allowance when chores are done, and help them save toward the things they really want — synced instantly between both parents' phones.

Features

Everything a family piggy bank should do

🧹

Allowance that teaches

Tie weekly or monthly allowance to a chore checklist. Kids check off their chores, then slide to get paid.

🎯

Savings goals

Save toward a LEGO set, a bike or a trip with progress bars — plus an optional parent match and interest.

💼

Family Job Board

Post jobs any kid can claim for extra cash. Ask for one off-schedule with Extra, and tip a job well done.

🤝

Real-world lessons

Lend your kids money and have it paid back from their allowance. Siblings can send money to each other.

📄

Monthly statements

A real bank-style statement for every month, saved as a PDF — and badges and streaks for good habits.

⌚

Everywhere you are

Home and Lock Screen widgets, an Apple Watch app with complications, and Siri: “Add money to Emma in Farrow.”

No ads, no trackingYour family's information is only used to run the app for your family.
You decide what kids can doKids can have their own login; you choose whether they can add money, delete their own entries, or see their siblings.
